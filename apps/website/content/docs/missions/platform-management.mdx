title: Platform management
description: Let an agent prepare missions in your name with the key of a mission on your Vaubaan account; it creates missions within your authority, asks you first by default, and never acts on itself.
---

An agent can prepare missions for other agents the way it does any other work: through a mission. Your **Vaubaan account** is your own place on the gateway — one click to connect, no secret, never shared — and a mission on it lets an agent call the `vaubaan__*` tools on the same endpoint as every service, `https://your-domain/mcp`, with that mission's key.

## Set it up

1. **Accounts → Connect a service → Vaubaan → Connect.** Nothing to create or paste.
2. On the account page, **Create the platform management mission**: the creation form opens prefilled with your Vaubaan account, every macro-action at Careful. Change anything, then create it.
3. Give the agent the key and the kit, as for any mission ([missions and agents](/docs/missions)).

At Careful the agent reads your accounts, the catalogs and your missions freely, and asks you before it issues a mission, replaces a key or stops a mission. The permissions, their rows and their limits are on the [Vaubaan plugin page](/plugins/vaubaan), like any service's.

## What the agent can do there

In this order: list the accounts you may put in a mission, read a service's permissions and macro-actions, preview a policy until it is valid, then issue the mission. The answer carries the mission key, shown once, and the kit for the agent that will do the work.

- It accepts explicit permissions only, copied from the catalog; no wildcard, never "everything". `everything_i_hold: true` is the one way to ask for all you hold on an account: the preview writes it out as the explicit list, and that list is what the mission stores.
- Every accepted permission has a limit per period and a rate.
- A permission outside your authority is refused, with the list of what is not covered.
- You are the creator of every mission it issues, and the mission is marked as prepared by the platform mission.

It can also list your missions, stop one, and replace the key of a mission it issued.

## What it cannot do

Answer a request (more of a limit, an approval), open a gate, extend a limit, reset a period, connect an account, give access, or call another service's tool. A mission it issues never holds a Vaubaan account, and it never replaces the key of its own mission nor stops it. Only your own key or a machine key you made calls these tools; a teammate's is refused. Those answers stay with you, in the interface.

Before 2026-11-14 agents used a second endpoint, `/mcp/manage`, authorized through OAuth; it now answers 404 with a hint, and the `oauth.*` configuration keys are ignored. More: the [Vaubaan plugin handbook](https://github.com/vaubaan/gateway/blob/main/docs/handbook/providers/vaubaan.md).
