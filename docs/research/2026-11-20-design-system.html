The line your agent works inside.

Vaubaan's design system, version 1. One accent, one shadow, four sizes in the app — and a vocabulary of risk, approval and refusal that every screen speaks the same way. shadcn's names for everything shadcn already names; Vaubaan's own tokens for the rest.

87 colour tokens152 contrast checks · 0 failingGeist 1.8 · self-hostedaccent #2F55C7
  1. 01A quiet control room. A page shows the exception, not the rule.
  2. 02One accent, blue: the primary action, links, and what waits for your answer.
  3. 03Green, grey, plum for risk; red only for what stopped. No orange, no yellow.
  4. 04Words before colour. Every state has its word and its shape.
  5. 05Drawn, not rendered. Hairlines, plan paper, no gradient, one shadow for what floats.

Trace: one bastion drawn in plan; its salient is the V. One 8-unit stroke on a 64 grid, so it holds at 16 px. Always one colour: ink on paper, paper on ink, white on the accent.
