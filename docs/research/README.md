# Research

Work that shaped the product and the site, kept whole: the form as well as the substance. Each page is the original, saved byte for byte from its published artifact; open it in a browser (it follows the light or dark theme). A Markdown transcription sits next to the long ones, for reading and searching without a browser.

These pages are evidence, not rules. What was decided from them lives in the intents, the specs, the ADRs and the style guide; when a page and those docs disagree, the docs win.

## Where the research lives in the harness

- **Profiles**: the human seats became six [profiles](../profiles/README.md). The evaluator merged into the developer and the consultant (it is a stage, not a person); the administrator and the account owner merged into the team lead (one person at six); the mission creator is the developer or the consultant; the agent is not a profile, its needs live in the intents and the spec.
- **Journeys**: each profile's journey is its level-1 intents, listed under it in the generated profiles index, and its pains; the holes T1–T13 and the façade's gaps G1–G18 are `proposed` intents.
- **Promise, pillars, competitor, where the value is born, moments M1–M7**: [the spec's vision](../spec/vaubaan-gateway.md#1-vision); the site's part in [website](../spec/website.md#1-vision).
- **Open questions**: Q3 and Q4 in [the spec](../spec/vaubaan-gateway.md#112-open-questions), Q5 in [plugin authoring](../spec/plugin-authoring.md#7-open-questions), Q6 in [website](../spec/website.md#5-open-questions). Q1 and Q2 are settled: desktop installers for macOS and Windows; the first sentence speaks to the person, the team right below.
- **Not held by the harness**: the journey grid of the façade (five surfaces, site, docs, README, app and agent, by six stages, with what exists and what is planned at each crossing), the time each moment takes today against its target, and the effort-versus-value map of plate 5. They stay in the façade and the table above.
- **Owner decisions since**: every example is given for two projects (a developer on GitHub and Supabase, a consultant on Gmail, Notion and Google Calendar); the risk scale's row 2 is named "Worth a look"; the brand is "Trace".
