# A teammate who works on a mission someone else created

A developer on a team whose lead runs Vaubaan. They were invited, often by a link they did not
expect, and work with their own agent on a mission a colleague created. The job: use the agent on
that mission without configuring anything. They create missions of their own too, and then wear the
developer's profile; this one holds what is specific to working under someone else's mission.
Desk research only; the façade's invited teammate and the table's teammate are the same seat.

## Pains

- **"I don't know what this is"** — the invitation leads to a sign-in page that does not say what Vaubaan is or who invited me.
- **Stuck when the creator is away** — only the creator answers a request; while they are on leave, mine expires and it looks like an outage.
- **Asking someone for a token** — getting access usually means a colleague hands over a secret.
- **Four copy-pastes per project** — the key shown once, a variable to export, a client block, an instruction block, a git helper.

## Gains

- **My key in one click** — generated by me, on the mission I was given, without asking anyone for a token.
- **Every call under my own name** — the activity shows what my agent did, not the creator's.
- **Nothing to configure** — the mission is already written; I only bring my agent.
