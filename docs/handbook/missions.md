# Missions — handbook

How a mission is previewed, issued, edited, shared with teammates and handed to an agent. Spec: [vaubaan-gateway](../spec/vaubaan-gateway.md) §0, §3.1–3.3 bis, §4.2, §4.4, §7.4–7.9 bis; who may delegate what: [authority](authority.md). ADRs: [resource-sets-partition-a-mission-connection](../decisions/resource-sets-partition-a-mission-connection.md), [macro-actions-and-trust-levels-prefill-the-policy](../decisions/macro-actions-and-trust-levels-prefill-the-policy.md), [preset-everything-i-hold-expands-explicitly](../decisions/preset-everything-i-hold-expands-explicitly.md), [resource-set-is-one-environment-with-optional-future-resources](../decisions/resource-set-is-one-environment-with-optional-future-resources.md), [created-resources-join-the-mission](../decisions/created-resources-join-the-mission.md), [mission-members-and-per-holder-tokens](../decisions/mission-members-and-per-holder-tokens.md), [mission-editable-after-issuance](../decisions/mission-editable-after-issuance.md), [mission-versions-kept-with-the-mission](../decisions/mission-versions-kept-with-the-mission.md), [permission-request-asked-by-the-agent](../decisions/permission-request-asked-by-the-agent.md), [mission-token-sha256-digest](../decisions/mission-token-sha256-digest.md).

## Start here

- [`mission-service.ts`](../../apps/gateway/src/server/missions/mission-service.ts) — every act on a mission: issue, preview, edit, gate, limits, rotate, revoke, `verifyToken`, `access`.
- [`preview.ts`](../../apps/gateway/src/lib/missions/preview.ts) — `previewDraft`, the pure decision behind the form, the `vaubaan__preview_policy` tool, `issue` and `edit`.
- [`macro-actions.ts`](../../apps/gateway/src/lib/missions/macro-actions.ts) — the account's macro-actions ("Anything else" added for the fallback) and the lines a trust level writes (risk model §4.6); the mission screen draws them through `missions/cards/` (below).
- [`everything-i-hold.ts`](../../apps/gateway/src/lib/missions/everything-i-hold.ts) — the built-in expansion and its one budget rule: the macro-action base budget at Autonomous, the catalog ceiling as rate, by row for a risk in no macro-action (spec §3.2; `RATE_BY_ROW`, `QUOTA_BY_ROW`: rows 1 to 4 keep the values of the retired low to critical).
- [`team-service.ts`](../../apps/gateway/src/server/missions/team-service.ts) — teammates and keys (member and named tokens).
- [`mission-reach.ts`](../../apps/gateway/src/server/store/mission-reach.ts) — what a mission reaches on an account: its stored lists, less stale and self.
- [`future-resources.ts`](../../apps/gateway/src/server/accounts/future-resources.ts) — the joins and leaves of future resources (`MissionsRepo.applyResourceChanges`); the automatic refresh (hourly and on open) and "check now" are in [accounts](accounts.md).
- [`bootstrap-kit.ts`](../../apps/gateway/src/server/missions/bootstrap-kit.ts) — what the agent is told.
- [`trust-raise.ts`](../../apps/gateway/src/server/missions/trust-raise.ts) — the count behind a trust-raise proposal (risk model §4.7); the offer is `components/missions/TrustRaiseOffer.tsx`, on the mission page and Now.
- [`versions.ts`](../../apps/gateway/src/lib/missions/versions.ts) — the History (spec §4.2): what a version keeps (`MissionVersionConfig`), sameness, its changes and their words, and `judgeRestore`, the restore bounded by the creator's authority now. The one writer is `recordSync` in [`mission-versions.ts`](../../apps/gateway/src/server/store/repositories/mission-versions.ts); `MissionService.history` / `restore` and [`history.ts`](../../apps/gateway/src/server/missions/history.ts) word it for `missions/[id]/history-list.tsx`.

## Invariants

- **One decision, run in the browser too.** `preview.ts`, `model.ts`, `catalog-view.ts`, `macro-actions.ts`, `summary.ts`, `everything-i-hold.ts`, `mission-edit.ts`, `token-name.ts` live under `src/lib/missions/` (with `lib/authority/catalog-authority.ts`, `lib/services/{links,planes,reference}.ts`, `lib/shares/restriction.ts`): the forms import them and show the server's own verdict. They import no value from `src/server/`, and a client component imports only types from there (**[inv: client-imports-no-server-value]**); `server/missions/index.ts` re-exports them for the server.
- **The persisted policy is the enumeration.** `issue` recomputes what the trust levels or `everything-i-hold` write, server-side, and stores explicit lines only: the row, the token, the `mission-issued` entry and the kit never name a macro-action, a trust level or `everything-i-hold`, so a later catalog or authority change cannot widen a mission.
- **Trust levels write a policy only when the draft gives none.** Without `policy` (an agent's `vaubaan__*` draft, an agent's proposal) `prefillOfTrustLevels` writes the lines and `left_out` says what it left out; beside explicit lines they decide nothing, and a line without a budget is `missing-budget`. The form therefore sends them only beside lines: an emptied account must not be refilled. `knownTrustLevels` keeps those of the account's macro-actions and `mission_resource_sets.trust_levels_json` stores them, per set, to redraw the screen: a macro-action reads its level only while its lines are exactly what that level writes on the card (`actionRows`), else **Custom** (a loaded list without trust levels reads Custom throughout). A trust level change alone is no edit (`hasChanges` ignores it).
- **Resource sets partition an account of a mission** (risk model §4.7). A draft account gives either the flat shape (`resources`, `future`, `env`, `policy`, `trust_levels`, `everything_i_hold`) or `resource_sets: [...]`, never both (`resource-sets-mixed`). Each resource is in one set (`resource-sets-overlap`). Stored: `mission_resource_sets` (id, `position` from 1, `future`, `env`, `mixed_env`, trust levels), `mission_resources.resource_set_id`, `mission_policy` keyed `(set, risk)`. A draft set's `id` is ignored at issuance; an edit keeps a set by id, or a single set to a single set (`keptSetOf`), and a set removed then re-added starts new counters.
- **A set is one environment** (ADR resource-set-is-one-environment-with-optional-future-resources). Every resource of a set carries the set's effective `env` (own label, else the account default), checked by `envOfSet` (`resource-set-mixed-env`) and again by the store (`InvariantError`). `env` is stored so that an empty set with `future` knows what it takes; a draft names it with `env` only when the list is empty. An unlabelled resource enters a set only where `needsEnvLabel` is false (no env-qualified risk, or the plugin derives the environment from the call): else `resource-unlabelled`. One set per environment at most takes future resources (`resource-sets-overlap`).
- **Legacy drafts are normalized, then previewed** (`normalizeSets` in `preview.ts`). `all` is the resources known now no other set selects, unlabelled ones left out where a label is needed (notice `unlabelled-resources-left-out`); `account` is `all` + `future`. The flat shape, `all` and `account` are split by environment (notice `split-by-environment`), each set keeping the unqualified lines and those qualified with its environment, trust levels and `everything_i_hold` copied; a qualified line of an environment no set holds stays in the first set, where it is `unresolvable-env`. A split set that accepts nothing and is refused for that alone is dropped (`settledSets`); a set given in `resource_sets` is never split. `judgeEdit` judges `normalizeDraft(draft)`, so that its draft sets and the preview's sets are one list.
- **A card is a resource set; the screen never says "set".** `missions/new/form-state.ts` holds `cards` (one account, the resources of one environment, `future`, trust levels, lines) and `draftOf` always sends `resource_sets`, one per card in screen order, `env` only for a card without resources, `{ scope: 'all' }` only for an account that lists no resource. Issues map back to a card by `account_id` + `resource_set_index` (its rank among the account's cards). The kit (`RESOURCE_SETS_LINE`) and the door's catalog still name sets ("Resource set N") when an account holds several; the management door's list views always carry `resource_sets`.
- **The platform management mission is a prefill, never an issuance.** `/missions/new?platform=<Vaubaan account id>` builds `platformMissionDraft` (`form-state.ts`): that account alone, every macro-action of its catalog but "Anything else" at Careful, the form's default period and end; the page previews it and opens the form on it like a copy (`InitialDraft.origin: 'platform'`). Nothing is issued until the person creates it. An account other than the reader's own Vaubaan account opens a blank form.
- **One screen, three uses.** `missions/new/mission-form.tsx` creates, edits (`/missions/<id>/edit`) and is what the mission page shows frozen. `missions/cards/card-model.ts` is the pure reading of a card (rows and their level or Custom, the Custom modal's rows and "never in <env>" rows, the step-2 groups of "+ Add an account", the gauge, the bar's change phrases); `cards/detail-cards.ts` builds the mission page's cards from `MissionDetail` — against the creator's issuance context, else against the mission's own lines: a teammate reads Custom for changed values or gates, but a permission the creator left out by hand is invisible to that reading, so the row may read a level where the creator reads Custom. Every word comes from a generic field (`resourceNoun`, catalog labels, plane slugs, env labels, `TRUST_LEVEL_LABELS` + `TRUST_LEVEL_CUSTOM`, `CARD_WORDS`): never write copy for one service there.
- **Step 2 groups by environment.** `resourceGroups`: labelled groups in the account's order, an empty group per catalog environment no resource carries yet (a card of future resources only), the unlabelled last and not selectable where `needsEnvLabel`. Hidden resources (by the owner, or by discovery: `hiddenByDefault`) are `ResourceGroup.hidden`, collapsed under "Hidden from new missions (n)" and never ticked by default, as `{ scope: 'all' }` leaves them out. Choosing a group's radio ticks its one free listed resource when it has exactly one, else nothing (`defaultTicked`; "Select all" ticks `freeIdsOf`), and unticks the previous group's; a resource held by another card, or a group whose future another card takes, is disabled ("in another card") — the engine would answer `resource-sets-overlap`.
- **A trust raise is offered, never applied.** `trustRaiseOffers` counts, per macro-action of a set with a stored trust level below Autonomous, the approval requests granted (approval or gate opened; never a claim) on a line of that set whose base risk id is in the macro-action, since the latest of: issuance, the last edit that changed that trust level (`mission_trust_marks.level_changed_at`, written by `MissionsRepo.edit`, and seeded by 0029 at the upgrade for missions issued before it), the last "Not now" (`snoozed_at`), the last approval refused on it. At `trust_raise.after_approvals` (default 10) the creator of a running mission sees the offer. "Raise" opens `/missions/<id>/edit?raise=<set>&action=<macro-action>`, honoured only when `trustRaiseOffer` still makes that offer; `raiseMacroAction` rewrites that macro-action's lines at the next level, keeps every other line, and the form lists the lines edited by hand it replaces. Saving is the ordinary audited edit.
- **One reach rule, three readers.** `reachedResources` is read at every request by the execution door (`loadMissionContext`), the mission detail and the kit, never cached, so the agent is never told it may touch what the door refuses.
- **Reach is the stored lists, nothing else.** `reachedResources` reads `resourceIds` only; no resource is resolved from the account at call time. A resource found later reaches a mission only by a write: `CreatedResources.record` (`addResources`, source `created`) or `MissionsRepo.applyResourceChanges` (source `future`), each audited on the mission (`mission-resources-added`, `mission-resource-joined`, `mission-resource-left`).
- **Future joins and relabel moves are event-driven.** `FutureResources.settle` diffs the account's rows around a write: a new id is `discovered`, an id whose effective env moved is `relabelled`. For each running mission (stored `active`, not past its expiry) with a set on the account: a relabelled resource leaves a set of another environment, then a resource joins the set with `future` on in its environment unless a set of the mission holds it, it is hidden, or it is the instance's own. A resource deselected by an edit therefore never comes back by itself; one that existed at issuance and was not ticked joins only if relabelled. Stopped missions move nothing.
- **Where a created resource goes.** Judged against one set: that set, whatever its environment. Several candidates: the one with `future` in the resource's environment, else one in its environment, else the first by position (`placementOf`) — never a set the call was not judged against. The edge: a resource created in another environment than its only candidate's stays in that set (it is reached), and the next edit refuses the set as `resource-set-mixed-env` until it is relabelled or moved. A held resource that appears in a discovery is never moved by it: only a relabel moves it.
- **Legacy sets (migration 0033).** A `account` set became the list of what it reached (hidden excepted, stale and self rows included and still unreached) with `future` on; every set got the environment of its resources. A set spanning several environments, or a `account` set with no resource yet, is `mixed_env`: `env` null, it takes a later resource of any environment (as the whole account did) and a relabel moves nothing out of it. It stays so until its next edit: drafted flat (one set) it is split by environment into new sets with new counters; inside `resource_sets` it is refused `resource-set-mixed-env`, so the edit screen splits it on load (`loadResolved`: one card per environment, each with the unqualified lines and those of its environment; the first keeps the set id and its counters) and says so (`split-by-environment` callout). `draftFrom` drafts a mixed future set as `{ scope: 'account' }`.
- **Creator-only mutations.** Every mutation goes through `ownMission`, which knows only the creator; `access` gives read to the creator and current members. "Not found" and "not yours" are one answer everywhere.
- **One membership rule.** `membershipOf` (`store/membership.ts`) is read by `access`, `list`, `standingOf` and inside `MissionsRepo.issueToken`'s transaction; it lives in the store because that transaction reads it.
- **A token's standing is read, not remembered.** `standingOf` re-reads holder status and membership on every call, so removal and deactivation take effect without a write. The writes on remove, deactivate and re-add exist so a returning member starts with no token rather than an old Bearer working again.
- **The secret exists in one answer.** Only the issue, rotate or `tokens/mine` answer to its holder carries it; the store keeps prefix and SHA-256 digest; the kit's `mcp_json` references `${VAUBAAN_MISSION_TOKEN}`. A test searches everything the creator can read, and every column, for a member's secret.
- **No store record leaves.** Store records are camelCase; what crosses a handler, a page prop or an audit payload is snake_case, translated only in `budget/mapping.ts` and `accounts/mapping.ts`. `issuedAuditOf` builds the `mission-issued` payload field by field (pinned by a test).
- **A refusal code needs its kit line.** `REFUSAL_GUIDANCE` is a `Record<RefusalCode, string>`, so a new code does not compile without one; `GET /agent-bootstrap.md` (`content.md`) is tested to document every code and to name no instance.
- **Every change of the configuration leaves one version, in its own transaction** (migration 0039). Each store write that may move a mission's configuration — `create`, `edit`, `changePermissions`, `addResources`, `applyResourceChanges`, `setGate`, `setLimits`, `replacePolicy`, `setExpiresAt`, and a claim's `setMissionExpiry` in `requests.ts` — ends with `versions.recordSync(missionId, stamp, now)` inside its `BEGIN IMMEDIATE`. It reads the tables as the write left them and appends only when the configuration differs from the latest version (`sameConfig`, order-blind, trust levels included), so a write that moves nothing leaves none. The stamp says the cause and the author: the person, the mission whose agent issued it through the `vaubaan` plugin (`issuedByMissionId`), or the gateway (a created resource, a future join or leave). Keys, members and counters are never in a version.
- **A restore is an edit** (`restore` → `judgeRestore` → `writeEdit`, shared with `edit`). The version's configuration is drafted against the mission now: an account the mission carries now and that no longer serves it (lost: not in the issuance context) is kept as it stands whatever the version holds of it, never removed nor rewritten; an account the creator no longer holds or revoked that the mission no longer carries, a resource gone, stale or the instance's own, a line outside their authority (then any line `judgeEdit` still refuses for its risk) are left out and returned as `dropped` sentences; a set the mission still has keeps its id and counters; the period is the mission's; an end already past keeps the current one. Written by `MissionsRepo.edit` with `restoredFrom`: the version it leaves is cause `rollback`, and the `mission-changed` audit entry carries `restored_from`. Creator only (`ownMission`), not on a revoked or orphaned mission; no confirmation.
- **A lost account stays until its creator removes it** (ADR [orphaned-missions-and-ownerless-connections](../decisions/orphaned-missions-and-ownerless-connections.md), amendment 2026-11-16). Nothing removes or hides it at any age. The mission page draws it as one red card folded to one line (`cards/lost-account-card.tsx`, words `explainLostAccount` in `server/missions/orphan-reason.ts`) in place of its set cards, which unfolding shows frozen; no banner, no mention in the header. `editView` returns `lost`; Edit mission draws the same card with "Remove from mission", which leaves the account out of the draft (`editDraftOf(…, removed)`), an ordinary edit (`accounts_removed`, one version). Without it, `editDraftOf` carries a lost account back as it is: an edit never removes what the creator did not see.
- **Creating and editing ask nothing** (spec §4.2: a confirmation only before an irreversible act). "Create mission" posts the draft at once and shows the key; "Save changes" posts the edit and lands on `/missions/<id>?edited=1` (`mission-edited` callout). The server re-judges both (`issue`, `edit` → `judgeEdit`); a 422 maps its issues back to the cards. The bar's change phrases (`changeSummary`) are the only recap.
- **Pages decide nothing.** An act is shown when the server would take it (`MissionTeam`, the state); `gates_editable` and `limits_editable` come from `gateEditBlockedBy`, the same rule `setGate` and `setLimits` apply (`rules-read-by-pages.test.ts`).

## Traps

- **Two transactions at issuance.** The mission row and its first token are written apart; a mission left without a token is repaired by `rotate`.
- **The token insert checks again.** Services check mission and caller before minting, but other requests run while hashing. `MissionsRepo.issueToken` re-reads mission status and membership in the `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` transaction that inserts, else throws `TokenNotIssuableError`. Keep both checks: the service alone knows derived states (`expired`, `orphaned`), the insert alone cannot be raced (`team-interleaving.test.ts`).
- **One account per service** stays although `mission_policy` is keyed `(set, risk_id)`: the resource stage finds the call's account by service, and migration 0029 attributes old lines and counters to a set by the risk's service prefix. Lifting it needs the account in the tool routing.
- **Policy maps are keyed by line.** `PolicyLineKey` (`<set>::<risk>`, `policyLineKey`, `lineKeyOf`) keys usage, limits and open gates everywhere a risk id alone was a key; a helper that takes a bare `RiskId` (`lineOfRisk`, `placeLines`) puts it in every set accepting it, else the first set of its service's account.
- **The environment label is the effective one** — the resource's own, else the account's `default_env` — applied by the repository on read; changing a default relabels every resource inheriting it.
- **A base id of an env-scoped risk** is valid in a macro-action (expanded per environment the selection resolves) but `unknown-risk` in an explicit policy.
- **The row orders, and pre-fills; nothing else.** A `CatalogRisk` carries `row` and `row_reason` (the policy engine's `rowOf` / `reasonOf` on the vector of its environment); `reason` is the catalog's prose, so the tree's branch is `row_reason` everywhere. Order with `byRowWorstFirst` / `hardestToRepair` (`catalog-view.ts`: row, then catalog order, `sort` being stable), never with a score. The expansion digest names `hardest` and `irreparable_auto` (row 4 at gate `auto`); a summary line quotes the row in lower case ("(cannot be repaired)").
- **`setGate` is single-process.** It reads the gate opening and writes the gate in two store calls; with a second process, move the decision into `MissionsRepo.setGate`'s transaction. It looks for the opening in the period running now even on an expired mission, else extending the expiry would revive it.
- **Wiring order.** The root builds missions before the `SessionService`, so `budget` defaults to `missionUsageReader(store.sessions)` — the same function, not a copy.
- **A trust level moved alone is no edit** (`hasChanges` ignores it), so it neither saves nor restarts a raise count; a raise always rewrites lines, so it saves.
- **A new writer of a mission's configuration must call `recordSync`** in its transaction, or the History misses a change and the next version's changes lump two together. A test per write path is in `tests/integration/missions/versions.test.ts`.
- **The backfill of 0039 is SQL** (`json_object` over the tables): what it writes must be what `recordSync` reads (`configNow`), or the first write after an upgrade leaves a version that moved nothing. `mission-versions-migration.test.ts` runs the shipped statement and compares; change both together.
- **History words are read at page load**: a version names accounts, resources and permissions by today's names (`historyWords`); one gone since reads "a former resource" or its id.
- **`changePermissions` has one screen**: `permissions-editor.tsx`, rendered by a permission request in Waiting for you. A hand edit of permissions is "Edit mission"; the mission page has no permissions page of its own (removed as a strict subset of the edit).
- **What an agent may do through the `vaubaan` plugin.** A mission rotates only the missions it issued (`issued_by_mission_id`; `not-issued-by-mission`), never itself; `edit` is not exposed there (`hard-deny.test.ts`); a mission it issues never holds a Vaubaan account ([services/vaubaan](services/vaubaan.md)).
- `preview(…, { assumedEnvs })` exists for the v4 importer's dry run ([import-from-v4](import-from-v4.md)); `issue` never takes it.
- **A flat draft on an account with two environments is two sets**: a call naming no resource is judged by both and charged in both (risk model §4.7). Tests about something else than sets select one environment.
- **The resource sets of the `vaubaan` plugin's views** are `resource_selections` (`future`, `env` per set); the legacy `resource_scope` is gone from every view and audit payload (`mission-issued`, `mission-changed` carry `future` and `env`).
- The screen never offers "Everything I hold" nor a "Select all" of permissions: a creator ticks macro-actions or Custom lines ("Select all" exists for the resources of a group only). `everything-i-hold` (an agent's draft) includes the fallback and names it in its notice.
- **Today's gauge** on the mission page follows the line closest to its limit (`gaugeOf`: highest share used), never an average; "Raise this limit" opens `/missions/<id>/edit?custom=<set>&action=<macro-action>`, the edit with that Custom modal open. "↻ Check now" on a card with `future` posts `/api/missions/<id>/resources/check` then refreshes the page.
- **"Ask me first" after issuance.** The mission page has no gate switch: a gate changes through Edit mission (Custom → behaviour), and an edit never closes a gate opened for the period. Closing one is in the hover of the gauge: a line open for the period says "runs freely until …" and, for the creator while `gates_editable`, offers "Close" (`cards/close-opening.tsx`), which posts `approval` to `/api/missions/<id>/gate` — `setGate` sees the opening and ends it (`closeOpening`), audited `gate-changed`. `detail-cards.ts` carries `openUntil` per line from `gate_open_until`.
- The teammate picker lists every active address on purpose: a free-text field would confirm or deny any address typed (threat model, A25).

## Debug

- **`account-unknown` on an existing account** → the reader is not its owner (or left the owning group), it is `revoked`, or its plugin is not registered (`root.registry.ids`).
- **Detail says "no resource" on an account that has some** → issued when it had none (or all hidden): it reaches none; edit the mission and pick them, or give the set `future` and let discovery bring later ones.
- **A resource found later did not join** → the set's `future` is off, its environment is another (`env` of the set vs the resource's effective label), it is hidden or the instance's own, another set of the mission holds it, or the mission is stopped; and discovery must have seen it appear (the automatic refresh, "check now", Refresh, after a creating call). The mission's audit shows every `mission-resource-joined` / `-left`.
- **`resource-unlabelled` on a mission that worked before** → its service has env-qualified risks: label the resource or give the account a default environment.
- **`risk-not-accepted` on one resource only** → the preview's `notices`: the resource has no label, or one no risk is acceptable in.
- **`unknown-risk` on `service.name`** → an env-scoped risk: write `service[env].name`.
- **"Needs an owner"** → derived per page load; reload after a re-share or a sign-in ([authority](authority.md)).
- **Edit answers `conflict`** → changed since opened (another tab, a permission answer): reload. **`period-not-editable`** → duplicate instead.
- **A key is refused** → `retired/rotated`, `revoked`, `holder-removed`, `holder-deactivated`, or `unknown` (another instance, truncated, not `vbn_live_`).
- **A member does not see the mission** → needs a `mission_members` row with `removed_at IS NULL` and an active user.
- **`mission-token-revoked` (`member-removed`) right after `mission-member-added`** → the re-added member still held an old working token.
